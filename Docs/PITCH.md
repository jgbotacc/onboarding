# Talk-only onboarding (`poc-voice`): pitch

**Just talk: answer a few questions out loud and say yes, and you're onboarded.**

## Who it is for

Small business owners who find reading or typing hard. They use a phone. They speak English, Chinese, Malay or Tamil.

## What is different

They finish onboarding by talking. The screen asks one question at a time out loud. Each answer is read back, and the owner says yes or no. Looks are described by name, and the owner picks by saying one, two, three or skip.

## Why it is smoother

Nothing to read through. Nothing to type. One question at a time, so there is no long form to face. Every answer is confirmed out loud before it counts, so mistakes are caught early. Saying a number is easier than tapping a small thumbnail.

## How it fits

It uses the same v2 backend. Answers go through the existing form and chat routes. The classic chat is still there at `#/chat`.
